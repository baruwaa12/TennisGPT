# Composure 90-Day One-Page Plan

## North Star

- Build a repeatable growth engine: `cold lead -> DM reply -> install -> paid founder -> retained subscriber`.
- Primary 90-day outcome: `300-500` paying founders with healthy retention.

## Fixed Offer (Do Not Change Weekly)

- Founder: `GBP 4.99/month` (first `200` users, locked forever).
- Regular: `GBP 9.99/month` or `GBP 59.99/year`.
- Guarantee: `7-day, 3-match full refund`.

## Monthly Milestones

- **Days 1-30:** `50-100` paying users, best DM script identified.
- **Days 31-60:** `150-250` total paying, retention loop improving.
- **Days 61-90:** `300-500` total paying, 1-2 reliable acquisition channels.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

- **Monday:** Film and prep content (2-3 short clips + 1 app demo).
- **Tuesday-Thursday:** Outreach blocks (new DMs + follow-ups + replies).
- **Friday:** KPI review and one funnel improvement.
- **Saturday:** Community post (Reddit/group) + collect user quotes.
- **Sunday:** Reset tracker, prep scripts, set next week targets.

## Daily Checklist (Tick Every Day)

- Sent `20-30` qualified first DMs (manual with light personalization).
- Sent follow-up batch for Day 2 non-responders.
- Sent follow-up batch for Day 5 non-responders.
- Posted one trust/proof asset (you playing + app insight).
- Logged every lead status in tracker.
- Reviewed objections from replies and updated scripts.
- Reviewed today’s numbers (reply, click, install, paid).

## Funnel KPIs (Track Weekly)

- DM reply rate.
- Link click rate from DMs.
- Install rate from clicks.
- Paywall view to purchase rate.
- Day-7 retention for new paid users.
- Refund rate under guarantee.

## Decision Rules (When Stuck)

- **Low reply rate (<10%):** Rewrite opener and improve profile credibility posts.
- **Good replies, low purchases:** Improve offer framing and paywall copy.
- **Good purchases, weak retention:** Build post-purchase habits (push reminders, weekly recap, next-match plan).
- **Channel underperforming for 2 weeks:** Shift source mix (clubs, coaches, groups, Reddit).

## Content Pillars (Reuse Every Week)

- Tactical mistake breakdowns (short and specific).
- Match-to-insight proof (`I played -> logged -> this is the adjustment`).
- Founder credibility posts (`built by a real player for serious players`).
- User quotes/results (as soon as available).

## Tooling (Lean Stack)

- Lead tracker: Notion/Airtable/Google Sheets.
- Landing page: Framer/Carrd.
- Form capture: Tally/Typeform.
- Analytics: GA4/Plausible.

## Hard Rules

- No risky mass DM bots.
- No changing pricing every few days.
- No relying only on friends for validation.
- No paid ads scaling until conversion + retention are stable.

## 5-Minute End-of-Day Review

- Where is the bottleneck today: reply, click, install, purchase, or retention?
- What single test will run tomorrow to fix that bottleneck?
- What exact number is tomorrow’s target for new DMs and follow-ups?
