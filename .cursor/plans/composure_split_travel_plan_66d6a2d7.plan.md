# Composure Split Plan (Pre-Departure + Thailand)

## How To Use This Plan

- Follow only the section for your current phase.
- Do tasks in order.
- Each task includes a short **why** so you stay focused.
- If overwhelmed, switch to the Freeze Mode at the end.

## Fixed Offer (Locked)

- Founder: `£4.99/month` (first `200` users, locked forever).
- Regular: `£9.99/month` or `£59.99/year`.
- Guarantee: `7-day, 3-match full refund`.
- CTA everywhere: `Become Founder`.

---

## Phase 1: Before You Leave (Now -> Next Week)

### Goal

Be fully setup before travel so Thailand is execution, not preparation.

### Instagram Objective (Pre-Store)

- Goal: build trust and warm audience before full store launch.
- Primary CTA in this phase: `Join Founder List` or `DM founder`.
- Why:
  - Pre-launch attention means faster conversions when app/store flow is fully ready.

### Task 1: Finalize Core Messaging Pack

- Action:
  - Keep one canonical founder statement.
  - Keep one CTA (`Become Founder`).
  - Keep one support contact.
- Why:
  - Consistent messaging increases trust and avoids confusion across app, DM, and landing page.

### Task 2: Build Lead Tracker

- Action:
  - Create tracker in Notion/Airtable/Sheet.
  - Required columns: handle, source, level, status, last contact, next follow-up, objection, notes.
  - Statuses: `new`, `messaged`, `replied`, `interested`, `installed`, `paid`, `no_response`.
- Why:
  - Without a tracker you lose follow-ups and waste outreach effort.

### Task 3: Create DM Script Pack

- Action:
  - Write 3 scripts: opener, qualifier, CTA.
  - Write 2 follow-ups: Day 2 and Day 5.
  - Save in one copy/paste location.
- Why:
  - Script consistency improves conversion and makes daily outreach faster.

### Task 4: Publish Landing Page + Domain

- Action:
  - Buy domain.
  - Publish one-page site with screenshots, founder offer, guarantee, CTA, FAQ.
  - Add analytics (GA4/Plausible).
- Why:
  - A single destination improves conversion and gives measurable funnel data.

### Task 5: Profile Trust Stack

- Action:
  - Update Instagram bio and link.
  - Pin 3 trust posts (founder story, app proof, tactical value).
  - Add story highlight: `Founder Offer`.
- Why:
  - Most DM prospects check profile first; trust stack lifts reply and click rates.

### Task 5B: Pre-Launch Instagram Content Cadence

- Action:
  - Publish `4` posts this week (screenshots, tactical tip, founder story, progress update).
  - Add one clear line to each post: `DM founder for early access`.
  - Keep one pinned post as your founder story.
- Why:
  - Consistent pre-launch posting builds credibility and creates an audience queue.

### Task 6: Soft Outreach Test

- Action:
  - Send `10-15` manual DMs.
  - Log every conversation.
  - Run at least one follow-up cycle.
  - Capture objections.
- Why:
  - You need baseline data before Thailand so you can improve faster there.

### Pre-Departure Definition of Done

- Offer is consistent across app/page/DMs.
- Landing page and analytics are live.
- DM scripts exist and are tested.
- Tracker has real lead data.
- First outreach batch completed.
- Instagram has at least 4 fresh posts and one founder story pin.

## Phase 2: Thailand Plan (Detailed Execution)

### Goal

Turn setup into daily growth and paid conversions.

### Instagram Objective (Thailand)

- Goal: convert warm audience + new viewers into paid founder users.
- Primary CTA in this phase: `Become Founder`.
- Why:
  - Instagram becomes a direct acquisition channel once your offer and funnel are live.

### Weekly Output Targets

- `3` short videos/week.
- `1` app demo clip/week.
- `20` new qualified DMs/day.
- Day 2 and Day 5 follow-up every day.
- `1` founder journey update/week.

### Weekly Operating Rhythm

#### Monday: Content Production Day

- Action:
  - Film/edit 3 short videos + 1 app demo asset.
  - Queue captions and CTA.
  - Prepare 1 founder-journey post (what improved this week).
- Why:
  - Batch production prevents daily content stress and keeps posting consistent.

#### Tuesday-Thursday: Outreach Days

- Action:
  - Send daily new DMs.
  - Run follow-ups.
  - Respond to replies and push to CTA.
- Why:
  - Daily outbound creates predictable lead flow and faster conversion learning.

#### Friday: Funnel Optimization Day

- Action:
  - Review KPIs: reply, click, install, paywall conversion, retention, refunds.
  - Pick one bottleneck and fix one thing only.
- Why:
  - Weekly iteration compounds results without overwhelm.

#### Saturday: Community Day

- Action:
  - Post one value-led community post (Reddit/group).
  - Collect objections/questions from comments/DMs.
- Why:
  - Community channels create trust and lower-cost acquisition.

#### Sunday: Reset Day

- Action:
  - Clean tracker.
  - Update scripts with best-performing lines.
  - Set next week DM and posting targets.
- Why:
  - Weekly reset keeps system organized and improves execution quality.

### Monthly Milestones (Thailand)

- Days 1-30: `50-100` paying users.
- Days 31-60: `150-250` total paying users.
- Days 61-90: `300-500` total paying users.

### KPI Rules (What To Fix)

- Low reply rate -> improve opener and trust profile.
- Good replies, low purchases -> improve offer framing/paywall copy.
- Good purchases, low retention -> improve usage habits and reminders.
- Weak channel for 2 weeks -> switch source channel.
- Good views, low DM replies -> strengthen post CTA and profile bio clarity.

### Blog/SEO Placement

- Start in Month 2 at 1 post/week.
- Why:
  - SEO is slow-compounding and should not delay short-term conversion work.

---

## Freeze Mode (Use On Hard Days)

- Do only this:
  1. Post 1 proof asset.
  2. Send 10 DMs.
  3. Track replies.
- Why:
  - Maintains momentum when motivation is low.

## End-of-Day Review (5 Minutes)

- What was today’s bottleneck: reply, click, install, purchase, retention?
- What single change will you test tomorrow?
- Tomorrow target: new DMs + follow-ups.
