# Composure Execution Bible

Use this as your single source of truth. Do not overthink. Follow it daily.

## 1) The Mission
- Build a repeatable engine: `lead -> reply -> install -> paid founder -> retained user`.
- Target for next 90 days: `300-500` paying founder users.
- Rule: consistency beats intensity.

## 2) Fixed Offer (Do Not Change Weekly)
- Founder: `£4.99/month` for first `200` users (locked forever).
- Regular: `£9.99/month` or `£59.99/year`.
- Guarantee: `7-day, 3-match full refund`.
- CTA everywhere: `Become Founder`.

## 3) Hard Rules
- No risky mass DM bots.
- No changing pricing every few days.
- No waiting for perfect content.
- No skipping tracking.
- No relying only on friends for validation.

## 4) Daily Minimum (Non-Negotiable)
- [ ] Send `20` qualified DMs (manual + light personalization).
- [ ] Send Day 2 and Day 5 follow-ups.
- [ ] Post 1 proof asset (screenshot/tip/app insight; video when available).
- [ ] Update lead tracker for every conversation.
- [ ] End-of-day 10-minute review.

If you only do these 5 things, you are winning.

## 5) Weekly Rhythm
- **Monday:** content prep (3 posts + 1 app demo asset).
- **Tuesday-Thursday:** outreach and follow-ups.
- **Friday:** KPI review + fix one bottleneck.
- **Saturday:** one community post (Reddit/group) + collect objections.
- **Sunday:** reset tracker and prep next week.

## 6) KPIs to Track Weekly
- DM reply rate.
- Link click rate.
- Install rate.
- Paywall view -> purchase rate.
- Day-7 retention for new paid users.
- Refund rate.

## 7) Decision Rules (What To Fix)
- Reply rate low -> improve opener + profile trust.
- Replies good, purchases low -> improve offer/paywall copy.
- Purchases good, retention low -> improve reminders + habit loop.
- No channel working for 2 weeks -> change source channel.

## 8) Tomorrow Plan (Day 1)
- [ ] Confirm founder offer text is identical in app, DMs, and notes.
- [ ] Verify paywall prices and legal links.
- [ ] Build/clean lead tracker.
- [ ] Prepare 3 DM scripts: opener, qualifier, CTA.
- [ ] Send first `10-20` DMs and log every result.

## 9) DM Script Starters
- **Opener:** `Quick one - I built Composure for tennis players who want tactical insights after matches. Open to trying founder access?`
- **Qualifier:** `What level are you playing at right now and how often are you matching up each week?`
- **CTA:** `Founder access is £4.99/month locked in (first 200). Want the link?`

## 10) If You Feel Overwhelmed
Do only this:
- 1 post
- 10 DMs
- track replies

Then stop. Repeat tomorrow.

## 11) End-Of-Day Review
- [ ] Bottleneck today: reply, click, install, purchase, or retention?
- [ ] One improvement for tomorrow:
- [ ] Tomorrow targets: new DMs __ / follow-ups __

Stay in execution mode. Small daily reps compound.
