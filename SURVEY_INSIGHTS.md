# TennisGPT Survey Analysis - Reddit User Feedback

> **37 responses** collected December 2025  
> **Source**: Reddit tennis community

---

## Executive Summary

### 🚨 Critical Finding: Product-Market Misalignment

**Current positioning**: AI Mental & Emotional Tennis Coach  
**What users actually want**: AI Tactical & Analytical Tennis Coach

**The mental/emotional features are your LEAST desired features.**  
**The tactical/analytical features are your MOST desired features.**

This requires a significant repositioning of the product.

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. REBRAND the App
**From**: "AI Mental Tennis Coach"  
**To**: "AI Tennis Strategist" or "AI Tactical Coach"

Lead with tactical analysis, not emotional support.

---

### 2. REPRIORITIZE Features

| Priority | Feature | Action |
|----------|---------|--------|
| 🥇 P0 | **Tactical Coach** | Make this the hero feature |
| 🥇 P0 | **Match Reflection** | Guided post-match analysis |
| 🥈 P1 | **Match Tracker** | Quick logging with AI insights |
| 🥉 P2 | Mental Check-In | Keep but de-emphasize |
| 🥉 P2 | Emotional Reset | Keep but de-emphasize |

---

### 3. REWRITE AI Prompts

**Current tone**: Supportive, encouraging, emotional  
**Needed tone**: Analytical, logical, strategic

Example change:
- ❌ "I understand how frustrating that must feel..."
- ✅ "Looking at this objectively, here's what the data shows..."

---

### 4. REFRAME the Value Proposition

**Old**: "Build mental resilience and emotional control"  
**New**: "Get expert tactical analysis and strategic insights after every match"

---

### 5. TARGET Audience Refinement

**Best fit users**:
- Advanced/Intermediate players (not beginners)
- Play 1-4x per week
- Play practice matches or compete
- Want to improve strategically
- Data/analytics minded

**Poor fit users**:
- Casual players who don't track matches
- Those seeking emotional support
- Beginners learning fundamentals
- Players whose issues are physical (fitness, courts, partners)

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Tactical > Mental**: Users want strategy, not therapy
2. **Analytical > Emotional**: 65% want logical tone
3. **Match Data > Daily Check-ins**: Logging matches beats mood tracking
4. **Healthy Skepticism**: ~15% don't believe an AI app helps
5. **Low Email Conversion**: Only 5% left emails for early access
6. **Experienced Users**: 86% intermediate or advanced

---

## The Bottom Line

> **Your users are analytical players who want strategic insights, not emotional support.**

The good news: You have the features they want (Tactical Coach, Match Tracker).  
The pivot: Lead with those, not Mental Check-In and Emotional Reset.

**Reposition from "AI Mental Coach" to "AI Tennis Strategist" and you align with what users actually want.**
